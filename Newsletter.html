  Summit Towing & Transport — Regional Update  

October Highlights: Building a Business, a Future, and a Creative Life

Summit Towing & Transport continues to evolve as I refine the long-term vision for the company. Every month brings new opportunities to strengthen the foundation, expand my technical skills, and build systems that will support future growth. This journey blends towing experience, education, creativity, and innovation into a single forward-moving path.

This month has been a powerful reminder that Summit Towing & Transport is only one part of a much bigger journey. While I continue laying the foundation for Summit across Sarasota County, Manatee County, and DeSoto County, I’m also pushing forward on my personal goals — working toward graduation, expanding my technical skills, and shaping the long-term systems that will support the business. Summit remains an independent vision built from the ground up, guided by real towing experience and a commitment to creating a dependable, modern operation for our local communities.

Beyond the trucks and transport planning, I’ve been developing a fully custom dispatch system designed to streamline communication, scheduling, and workflow for towing operations. This project blends my industry experience with my growing knowledge in coding, wiring, and system design. At the same time, I’ve been expanding my creative side through 3D printing, electronics projects, and building new content for my YouTube channel. These projects let me explore the maker side of who I am — the part that loves creating, experimenting, and learning new skills.

All of this reflects something important: towing is a major part of my life, but it’s not the whole story. I’m a builder, a creator, a student, and a problem-solver. Whether it’s shaping Summit’s future, finishing my education, designing new tech, or making something fun on the 3D printer, every piece contributes to the bigger picture. With continued support from my wife on the administrative and financial side, we’re building not just a business — but a future with depth, creativity, and purpose.
